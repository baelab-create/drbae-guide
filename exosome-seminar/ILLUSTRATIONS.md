# Scientific illustration production notes

Created with the built-in image generation tool. All illustrations are conceptual; none is a microscopy image or product validation result.

1. BBB research: 3D capillary lumen, endothelial junctions, surrounding neural support cells and a targeting-vesicle inset. No claim that all vesicles cross the BBB.
2. Liposome / EV: side-by-side phospholipid bilayer vesicles, designed payload versus diverse biological cargo. No superiority claim.
3. Lyophilization: hydrated vesicle, protective sugar-glass environment and rehydrated vesicle. Do not depict inevitable destruction and reassembly.
4. Capillary groove: a wetted open microgroove and meniscus close-up. No human skin, no puncture, no actual product geometry.

Shared direction: professional biomedical journal illustration; pearl grey background; Dr.BAE blue #2F80D0; restrained pink, sage and gold; precise material rendering; no generated labels or data.
